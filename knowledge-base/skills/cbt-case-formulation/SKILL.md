# CBT case formulation (conversation craft)

## What This Is
Phase One: guide the user to see how a concrete situation links to thoughts, feelings/body, and what they do or avoid — using their words. Phase Two: process-based goals (controllable fairness/professionalism), max 5 steps with 2 micro-actions each, including stimulus control when phone/scrolling avoidance shows up.

## How to use (coach-facing)
- Reflect disclosed pieces first; do not jump to solutioning.
- Normalise imposter/fraud feelings briefly when present.
- Ask one Socratic question on the next missing link; say why that moment matters.
- Never invent named people or workplace facts the user did not share.
